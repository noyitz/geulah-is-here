Our story

Embracing Our Jewish Journey in the Santa Cruz Mountains

In 2015, our family embarked on a transformative journey to the scenic Santa Cruz Mountains, seeking a change that would redefine our lives and deepen our sense of purpose. Little did we know the profound impact this decision would have on our connection to our Jewish roots and our entire family's spiritual growth.

As the years unfolded, we delved deeper into our heritage, immersing ourselves in Jewish traditions, teachings, and practices. Our thirst for knowledge led us to discover a newfound connection to our faith and a profound love for our rich cultural heritage.

In recent years, as we continued our personal journey of self-discovery, we made a conscious decision to give back to our community. We recognized that our experiences and knowledge could serve as a guiding light for those seeking a closer relationship with our Creator.

Today, we stand committed to the mission of bringing people closer to their Jewish heritage. Through our various offerings, from cost-free weddings and bar mitzvah services to inclusive Shabbat gatherings and transformative women's weekends, we strive to create a nurturing space where individuals can explore their Jewish identity, learn, and find solace amidst the majestic beauty of the Santa Cruz Mountains.

Welcome to our story, where love, knowledge, and a sense of purpose intertwine to shape a brighter future for all.
