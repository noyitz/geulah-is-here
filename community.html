Community Programs

Jewish learning, nature, and community for every age.

We welcome schools, camps, preschools, after-school groups, families, and community organizations for meaningful Jewish experiences in the Santa Cruz Mountains.

Our community programs create a warm space where children, teens, and families can connect to Jewish heritage through hands-on learning, outdoor experiences, celebration, and shared meals.

Programs can be shaped for local Jewish schools, camps, preschools, after-school programs, enrichment groups, homeschool groups, and family gatherings. We combine Jewish education with the natural beauty of the redwoods so every visit feels grounded, joyful, and memorable.
