Support Our Soldiers

Donate books for wounded heroes and warriors in battle.

Our soldiers put everything on the line to protect us. Whether recovering from serious injuries in rehabilitation centers or standing strong on the front lines, they need our support, both physically and mentally.

We are launching a special initiative to provide books to our wounded soldiers in recovery and to those actively serving on the battlefield. Books offer comfort, strength, and inspiration in times of challenge.

The book we are distributing is To Win a War - The Jewish Way, which explores the resilience and strategy that have defined Jewish survival and victory throughout history. This book is more than just reading material. It is a source of wisdom and empowerment for those who fight to protect our people today.

  • Donate: Contribute funds to help us purchase copies of To Win a War - The Jewish Way and other books that match each soldier's needs, whether in rehabilitation or on active duty.
  • Spread the Word: Share this initiative with your friends, family, and community to help us reach more people.

Every book we give is a message of support and strength. Let's stand together and ensure our soldiers, both in recovery and on the battlefield, receive the inspiration they deserve.
