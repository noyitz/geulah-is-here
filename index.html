Our mission

Creating meaningful Jewish experiences in nature.

Welcome to our non-profit Jewish organization, dedicated to providing a range of exceptional services that celebrate our rich heritage and foster a vibrant community. From cost-free weddings and bar mitzvah services to inclusive Shabbat gatherings amidst serene redwood trees, we offer meaningful experiences that embrace tradition and create lasting memories.

Join us for our joyful bi-monthly Hafrashat Challah events, where we come together to bless and share in the joy of challah. Our transformative women's weekends provide a nurturing space for connection, learning, and relaxation, while our venue opens its doors to local Jewish schools and camps for engaging field trips.

Inspiring Jewish education meets thrilling nature adventures at our teen events, cultivating a sense of pride and discovery. Our Jewish holiday services create an immersive and festive atmosphere, extending an open invitation to our community, friends, and travelers to partake in the very best of holiday celebrations.

Our services

Hospitality, learning, celebration, and connection.

Weddings

Enjoy a cost-free wedding at our venue as we gladly offer our property for hosting your Jewish ceremony.

Bar Mitzvah

Join us as we graciously host bar mitzvah services, providing a welcoming space for this significant milestone celebration.

Shabbat Services

Experience an inclusive Shabbat service surrounded by majestic redwood trees, complete with accommodation, a delicious Shabbat dinner, and a peaceful Sabbath.

Hafrashat Challah

Come and join us for our bi-monthly Hafrashat Challah event, where we gather to separate and bless the challah dough in a joyous setting.

Women's Retreats

Experience a transformative women's weekend with Shabbat service, engaging lectures, camaraderie, and relaxation.

Field Trips

We warmly welcome field trips from local Jewish schools and camps, providing an engaging and educational experience.

Teen Events

Embark on inspiring Jewish education and exciting nature adventures, blending knowledge and exploration for an unforgettable experience.

Holiday Services

Embrace the finest holiday experience as we host Jewish holiday services for our community, friends, and travelers.
