Weddings & Bar Mitzvahs

No-charge Jewish celebration hosting for families who need support.

We open our property for meaningful Jewish weddings and bar mitzvah celebrations so families can mark these sacred milestones with dignity, warmth, and community.

Geulah is Here offers our venue without charge for Jewish weddings and bar mitzvahs for families who need support. Our goal is to remove financial pressure from families who want to celebrate an important Jewish milestone in a beautiful, welcoming, and meaningful setting.

The redwoods create a peaceful backdrop for ceremony, prayer, family gathering, and celebration. We work with each family to understand what they need and what kind of event will feel appropriate, respectful, and joyful.

This offering is made possible through community generosity. Donations help us maintain the property, prepare the space, support hospitality, and keep these celebrations accessible to families who otherwise may not be able to host them.
