Tambourines - תופי מרים

Spreading the Joy of Redemption

A call for the return of our captives.

Just as Miriam the Prophetess led the women of Israel in song and dance after the miraculous parting of the Red Sea, we too raise our voices, praying and acting for the complete redemption of our people, including the safe return of our hostages.

We are launching a special initiative to distribute tambourines at women's gatherings, reviving Miriam's unwavering faith in salvation. These tambourines symbolize not only joy but also our determination to see every Jew return home. Just as we left Egypt together and carried Joseph's remains with us, we will not rest until every hostage is brought back.

Each tambourine is dedicated to:

  • The victims of October 7, whose names form the sea in a unique artwork.
  • The fallen soldiers, whose names are woven into a lion, symbolizing bravery and strength.
  • The hostages held by Hamas, represented in the figures crossing the sea, just as we carried Joseph's remains out of Egypt, vowing never to leave a Jew behind.

Miriam prepared drums in anticipation of redemption because she believed with certainty that salvation would come. Today, we take inspiration from her faith and act with the same conviction. Every tambourine we distribute is a call to bring our captives home.

How You Can Take Part

  • Donate: Help us purchase and distribute tambourines to women's gatherings, raising awareness and prayers for the release of our hostages.
  • Join the Movement: Bring this initiative to your community and make the call for our captives louder.
  • Spread the Message: Share this mission and ensure that no one forgets those still in captivity.

May the sound of these tambourines echo our prayers, shake the heavens, and bring about the safe return of every hostage.
